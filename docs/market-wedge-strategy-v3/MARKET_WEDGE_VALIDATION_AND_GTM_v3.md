# Market Wedge Validation and GTM v3

## Wedge hypothesis

UK bid agencies serving SME clients will pay for a secure workspace that reduces client evidence chasing, speeds up bid qualification, and produces evidence-backed first drafts.

## Interview targets

- 10 bid agencies
- 10 SMEs in chosen vertical
- 5 in-house bid managers
- 5 public-sector sales/capture people

## Questions

1. How many tenders do you review each month?
2. How many do you bid?
3. How do you decide bid/no-bid?
4. What tools do you currently use?
5. How do you manage client evidence?
6. What evidence is usually missing?
7. How do you build compliance matrices?
8. How do you reuse previous answers?
9. Who reviews final answers?
10. What causes late-stage submission stress?
11. What would this need to replace?
12. What would make this unsafe?
13. What would you pay for this?
14. Who signs off on buying it?

## Pilot offer

We help you qualify real UK public-sector opportunities for one client, identify missing evidence, generate a compliance matrix, and produce evidence-backed first drafts using the client’s approved documents.

## Pilot pricing hypothesis

- £500–£1,500 per pilot
- £500/month design partner
- £1,000–£3,000 supported bid workflow

## Early outputs

- bid readiness report
- evidence gap report
- compliance matrix
- bid/no-bid memo
- client evidence request
- bid pack export
