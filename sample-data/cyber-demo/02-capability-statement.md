## Core Capability Areas

### 1. Cyber Essentials & Cyber Essentials Plus Assessment

We hold NCSC Certification Body status, enabling us to assess and certify organisations against the UK government's Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus schemes. Our assessors hold current IASME Assessor accreditation and are re-validated annually.

- Organisations assessed to date: 183 (as at April 2024)
- Failure rate on first submission: 28% (below national average of 34%)
- Average turnaround (submission to certificate): 8 working days
- Sectors covered: NHS, local authority, education, housing, legal, manufacturing

### 2. Penetration Testing

All penetration testing is conducted by CREST-certified or CHECK-listed testers. We do not subcontract testing engagements without prior client consent.

Scope of capability:

- Web application penetration testing (OWASP Top 10, PTES methodology)
- Infrastructure testing (internal and external, wired and wireless)
- Social engineering (phishing simulation, vishing, physical)
- Mobile application testing (iOS and Android)
- OT/ICS network assessments (Purdue model, IEC 62443 alignment)
- Red team exercises (adversarial simulation, assumed breach)

Average contract value: £12,000–£35,000
Largest single penetration test contract: £78,000 (regional NHS ICB, 2023)

### 3. Incident Response

We maintain a 24/7 IR retainer service with a guaranteed 2-hour response SLA for retained clients and a best-endeavours 4-hour response for non-retained engagements.

- Annual retainer clients: 11 (as at April 2024)
- IR engagements handled (lifetime): 47
- Types: ransomware (22), BEC/email compromise (11), insider threat (6), malware/backdoor (5), data exfiltration (3)
- Average time to containment (retained clients): 5.8 hours

### 4. Security Awareness & Training

We design and deliver bespoke security awareness programmes. All phishing simulation campaigns are conducted via our licensed KnowBe4 partner portal.

- Phishing simulation campaigns delivered: 94 (across 31 client organisations)
- Bespoke e-learning modules created: 12
- Average click-rate reduction after 6-month programme: 67%

### 5. Virtual CISO (vCISO)

Fractional CISO provision for organisations that require strategic security leadership without the cost of a full-time hire.

- Current vCISO clients: 9
- Typical engagement: 2–4 days per month
- Day rate: £1,100–£1,350 (depending on seniority)
- Deliverables: risk register maintenance, board reporting, supplier assurance, policy governance, audit liaison

### 6. Compliance & Governance Consultancy

- GDPR / UK GDPR gap analysis and remediation
- Data Security & Protection Toolkit (DSPT) — NHS Organisations
- ISO 27001 readiness assessment and implementation support
- NIS Regulations gap analysis
- Supply chain security review (Cyber Essentials upstream supplier assurance)
