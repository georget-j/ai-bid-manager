# Fortis Cyber Solutions Ltd — Certifications and Accreditations

## Summary

This document lists all organisational and individual certifications held by Fortis Cyber Solutions Ltd, including renewal dates, accrediting bodies, and the services each certification enables.

---

## Organisational Certifications

### Cyber Essentials Plus Certification Body (NCSC / IASME)

- **Status:** Active
- **Accrediting body:** IASME Consortium (on behalf of NCSC)
- **Renewal date:** 31 January 2025
- **Scope:** Fortis is authorised to assess and certify organisations against both Cyber Essentials (self-assessment) and Cyber Essentials Plus (verified assessment) schemes.
- **Significance:** Enables us to issue CE and CE+ certificates with NCSC backing; a requirement for most public sector procurement that mandates CE/CE+.
- **Assessor-in-charge:** Sarah Hartley (primary), Ben Whitmore (secondary)
- **Annual assessment volume (2023/24):** 61 organisations assessed

### CREST Member Organisation

- **Status:** Active member
- **Accrediting body:** CREST International
- **Renewal date:** Annual (March)
- **Scope:** CREST membership enables us to market and deliver penetration testing, red team, and threat intelligence services under the CREST quality framework.
- **Individual CREST certifications held:** see individual certifications below
- **Required for:** HMG CHECK engagements (alongside CHECK listing); NHS Digital penetration testing; many MOD-supply-chain requirements

### CHECK Service Provider

- **Status:** Listed
- **Accrediting body:** NCSC (via application and CREST sponsorship)
- **Listing reference:** CHCK-2021-0093
- **Scope:** Authorises Fortis to deliver IT Health Checks (ITHCs) for HMG departments, Arms-Length Bodies, and Crown-related bodies.
- **Requirement:** All testing delivered under CHECK must use CHECK Team Leaders (CTLs) and CHECK Team Members (CTMs). Fortis holds 1 CTL and 2 CTMs.

### IASME Gold

- **Status:** Certified
- **Accrediting body:** IASME Consortium
- **Certificate number:** IASME-G-2023-0441
- **Renewal date:** November 2024
- **Scope:** IASME Gold demonstrates that Fortis itself meets a comprehensive set of cyber security and data protection controls, including governance, risk management, business continuity, and supplier management — beyond the technical controls of CE+.
- **Significance:** Required for IASME Consortium membership and differentiator for SME public sector bidding.

### ISO 27001 — In Progress

- **Status:** Gap assessment completed; implementation in progress
- **Target certification:** Q3 2025
- **Certification body (selected):** BSI
- **Current stage:** Stage 1 (documentation review) complete; Stage 2 surveillance audit scheduled Q2 2025
- **Gap assessment conducted by:** External assessor (not Fortis)
- **Key outstanding actions:** Asset register finalisation; formal risk treatment plan sign-off; internal audit cycle to complete

## Notes

- All individual certifications are verified annually at appraisal. Staff are contractually required to notify Fortis within 30 days of any certification lapse or revocation.
- CREST individual certifications require re-examination every 3 years. Fortis funds re-examination for all relevant staff.
- The ISO 27001 pursuit is driven by two factors: (a) increasing requirement in public sector tenders, (b) the desire to demonstrate that our own security posture matches the standards we recommend to clients.
