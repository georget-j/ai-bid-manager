# Fortis Cyber Solutions Ltd — Company Overview

## About Us

Fortis Cyber Solutions Ltd is a specialist cyber security consultancy headquartered in Leeds, West Yorkshire. Founded in 2018 by two former GCHQ-linked intelligence analysts, we support small and medium-sized organisations across the UK in understanding, managing, and reducing their cyber risk.

We employ 20 full-time professionals — including certified penetration testers, incident responders, governance consultants, and security awareness trainers — and operate from our Leeds office with field teams covering Yorkshire, the North West, and the Midlands.

Our clients range from GP practices and housing associations to regional law firms and tier-two manufacturers. We are deliberately focused on the SME and public-sector mid-market: organisations that face real cyber threats but cannot afford the retainer model of a large MSSP.

## Core Services

- **Cyber Essentials & Cyber Essentials Plus** — We are an NCSC-accredited Certification Body. We have assessed and certified over 180 organisations since 2019.
- **Penetration Testing** — Web application, infrastructure, and social engineering assessments. CHECK and CREST certified.
- **Incident Response** — 24/7 on-call IR retainer and ad hoc breach response. Average containment time: 6 hours.
- **Security Awareness Training** — Bespoke phishing simulation and e-learning programmes.
- **Virtual CISO (vCISO)** — Fractional CISO service for organisations without in-house security leadership.
- **Compliance Consultancy** — GDPR/UK GDPR gap analysis, DSPT assessments for NHS bodies, ISO 27001 readiness.

## Why Clients Choose Fortis

1. **Plain-language communication** — We do not use jargon with non-technical boards.
2. **Proportionate advice** — Our recommendations are sized for SME budgets, not enterprise procurement.
3. **Continuity** — Clients deal with the same named consultant throughout their engagement; we do not rotate junior staff.
4. **Speed** — CE/CE+ assessments completed within 10 working days of application. IR retainer clients guaranteed a response within 2 hours.
5. **Independence** — We are vendor-neutral. We do not resell licences or earn commission on third-party products.
